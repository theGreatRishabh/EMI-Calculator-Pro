Home ownership in Germany and the wider EU often comes with strict lending rules but excellent incentives for sustainability and investment. If you are looking to buy property in the Eurozone, here are the key financial factors to consider.

## 1. Interest Deduction for Investment Properties

In Germany (and many other EU nations), mortgage interest is a tax-deductible expense for **investment properties** (Kapitalanlage).

- **Full Deduction:** 100% of the interest paid on your loan can be deducted from your rental income.
- **Linear Depreciation:** You can also deduct 2-3% of the building's cost (excluding land) annually as depreciation (AfA).
- **Strategy:** This makes real estate one of the most tax-efficient investment vehicles in Germany.

## 2. KfW Energy Efficiency Bonuses

The *Kreditanstalt für Wiederaufbau* (KfW) offers specialized low-interest loans and repayment bonuses for energy-efficient homes.

- **Repayment Bonus:** Depending on the efficiency standard (e.g., KfW 40 Plus), you may receive a grant of up to **€15,000 - €37,500** that you don't have to pay back.
- **Low Interest:** KfW loans often feature interest rates significantly lower than standard commercial bank rates.

## 3. KfW Home Ownership Programme (124)

Even if your home isn't ultra-energy-efficient, the KfW 124 program provides low-interest loans specifically for buying a home.

- **Loan Amount:** Up to **€100,000** per project.
- **Interest-Only Years:** You can opt for an initial period (tilgungsfreie Zeit) where you only pay interest, helping you manage cash flow in the first few years of home ownership.

## 4. Maintenance and Renovation Costs

For older properties, the government allows significant deductions for "modernization" measures. If you renovate to improve energy efficiency or accessibility, these costs can often be written off against your income tax over several years.

## 4. Speculation Tax

In Germany, if you sell a property after holding it for at least **10 years**, the capital gains are tax-free. If it was your primary residence, this period is reduced to just 2-3 years.
