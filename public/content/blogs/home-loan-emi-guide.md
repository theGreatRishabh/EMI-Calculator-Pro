A home loan is often the largest financial commitment you will make. This guide breaks down exactly what you need to know to minimize your interest burden.

## 1. Understand the True Cost of Your Loan

When you borrow ₹50,000,000 for 20 years at a 9% interest rate, you don't just pay back the principal. By the end of the tenure, you will have paid nearly as much—or more—in interest as the original loan amount. Your total repayment might exceed ₹10,000,000.

Understanding the Amortization Schedule (how your payments are split between principal and interest over time) is the first step in planning.

## 2. The Impact of Loan Tenure

Many borrowers opt for a longer tenure (e.g., 25 or 30 years) to reduce their monthly EMI burden. While this makes the monthly budget easier to manage, it drastically increases the total interest paid.

- **Short Tenure:** Higher EMI, much lower total interest.
- **Long Tenure:** Lower EMI, much higher total interest.

> **💡 Pro Tip:**
> Always choose the shortest tenure you can comfortably afford without compromising your emergency fund or daily expenses.

## 3. The Power of Prepayments

The secret to beating a home loan is regular prepayments. Making just one extra EMI payment per year can shave off roughly 3-4 years from a 20-year loan tenure. Because interest is charged on the outstanding principal balance every month, any amount paid extra goes directly to reducing the principal.

## 4. Floating vs. Fixed Interest Rates

In a high-interest-rate environment, locking into a fixed rate might seem attractive. However, historically in many regions, floating rates end up being cheaper over a 15-20 year horizon. When repo rates fall, your EMI or tenure will drop automatically.
