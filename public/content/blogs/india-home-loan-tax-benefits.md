Buying a home in India is not just a milestone; it's a significant financial strategy. The Government of India provides several tax incentives to encourage home ownership, primarily through the Income Tax Act.

## 1. Section 24(b): Deduction on Interest

The most substantial benefit comes under Section 24(b). You can claim a deduction of up to **₹2,00,000** per financial year on the interest portion of your home loan EMI for a self-occupied property.

- **Limit:** ₹2 Lakhs per annum.
- **Condition:** The construction must be completed within 5 years from the end of the financial year in which the loan was taken.
- **Pro Tip:** If you have a let-out property, the entire interest is deductible without any upper limit (though loss from house property is capped at ₹2 Lakhs against other heads).

## 2. Section 80C: Deduction on Principal Repayment

The principal component of your EMI is eligible for deduction under Section 80C.

- **Limit:** Up to ₹1,50,000 (shared with other investments like LIC, PPF, and ELSS).
- **Condition:** You must not sell the property within 5 years of possession, or the tax benefit will be reversed.

## 3. Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) 2.0

The Government has recently introduced **PMAY 2.0** to further support urban and rural housing.

- **Interest Subsidy:** Eligible middle-income and low-income families can receive an interest subsidy on their home loans, drastically reducing the effective interest rate.
- **CLSS Benefit:** Under the Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme, the subsidy is credited upfront to the loan account, reducing the principal and, consequently, the EMI.
- **Women Empowerment:** The scheme encourages house ownership in the name of the female head of the family or in joint names.

## 4. Section 80EEA: For First-Time Home Buyers

For "Affordable Housing," first-time buyers can claim an additional ₹1,50,000 deduction on interest, provided the property value is below ₹45 Lakhs. This is over and above the ₹2 Lakhs limit of Section 24(b).

## 4. Stamp Duty and Registration Charges

You can also claim a deduction for stamp duty and registration charges under the Section 80C limit (₹1.5 Lakhs) in the year these expenses are incurred.
