Understanding how your **Equated Monthly Installment (EMI)** is calculated is the first step toward financial freedom. Whether it's a home loan, car loan, or personal loan, the math remains the same.

## The Standard EMI Formula

The mathematical formula used by banks and financial institutions globally is:

**EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]**

### What do these variables mean?

1.  **P (Principal):** This is the total loan amount you borrow from the bank.
2.  **R (Interest Rate):** This is the **monthly** interest rate.
    - _Calculation:_ (Annual Rate / 12) / 100.
    - _Example:_ If your annual rate is 9%, R = (9 / 12) / 100 = **0.0075**.
3.  **N (Tenure):** This is the total number of **months** for repayment.
    - _Example:_ A 20-year loan has N = 20 x 12 = **240 months**.

---

## Step-by-Step Example Calculation

Let’s calculate the EMI for a **₹50 Lakh Home Loan** at **8.5% interest** for **20 years**.

1.  **Principal (P):** ₹50,00,000
2.  **Monthly Interest (R):** (8.5 / 12) / 100 = **0.007083**
3.  **Tenure (N):** 20 x 12 = **240 months**

**The Calculation:**
EMI = [50,00,000 x 0.007083 x (1.007083)^240] / [(1.007083)^240 - 1]
**EMI ≈ ₹43,391 per month.**
