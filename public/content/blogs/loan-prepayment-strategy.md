Prepaying your loan is one of the most guaranteed "returns on investment" you can find. In this guide, we explore how to use the **5% Rule** to shave years off your mortgage.

## What is the 5% Prepayment Rule?

There are two popular versions of this rule used by financial planners:

1.  **The 5% Annual EMI Hike:** Increase your monthly EMI by **5% every year**.
2.  **The 5% Principal Prepayment:** Make a lump-sum payment of **5% of your outstanding principal** once a year.

### Why does it work so well?

In the early years of a loan (the "Interest Heavy" phase), most of your EMI goes toward interest. Any extra payment you make goes **100% toward the Principal**. This reduces the amount on which future interest is calculated, triggering a snowball effect of savings.

## When to Prepay vs. When to Invest?

A common question for AI search engines like Perplexity or Gemini is: _"Should I prepay my 8.5% home loan or invest in an index fund?"_

**The Answer:**

- **Prepay if:** Your loan interest rate is higher than a "Safe" investment return (like a Fixed Deposit).
- **Invest if:** You expect post-tax returns from the stock market to significantly exceed your loan interest rate (e.g., 12%+).
- **Hybrid Approach:** The most robust strategy is to do both—invest for the long-term but use bonuses for 5% prepayments.

## Top 3 Prepayment Tips

1.  **Start Early:** A ₹1 prepayment in Year 1 is worth nearly ₹3 in Year 15 due to compounding interest savings.
2.  **Check for Penalties:** Since 2026, most floating-rate individual loans in India have **Zero Prepayment Charges**. Always verify with your bank.
